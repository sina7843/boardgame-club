## 2. Shared frame (`apps/web/src/pages/Table.tsx`, `apps/web/src/styles/table.css`, `packages/ui/src/dock.tsx`)

- `FrameView` layout:
  - Desktop: rail (inline-start), then the felt (opponent bar or seat strip, stage, dock row, own bar), then chat.
  - Phone: game bar, then a one-line status (`<details>`; the rail hints open from it), then the felt, then the dock.
- Renderers plug into the frame through `useInFrame()`, `<RailSlot>` (rail cards and hints) and `<DockSlot>` (action buttons).
  - `GROUP_FRAME` assigns each game `duel` (two players) or `seats` (strip).
- Stage sizing:
  - `.gf-stage > *` takes the full width, and `.zb` (ZoomBoard) takes the full width too.
  - This fixed the collapsed boards in amlak, ludo, risk, snakes, battleship, TTR and quoridor.
- Clocks: every seat shows a clock. Idle seats show the turn length.
- The phone felt scrolls instead of clipping.
- `.visually-hidden` utility (app-wide).
- New this round:
  - The game bar meta shows the player count («۳ بازیکن»).
  - `.gf-stage` carries `data-rev` (the game revision) as a test hook.

## 7. e2e triage

The causes fell into three groups:

1. **Tests read renderer panels that the frame now hides.** These moved to frame hooks (`.gf-seat`, `.gf-stage--mine`, `data-rev`):
   - coup, scout, risk, TTR, catan, el-dorado, sea-salt-paper, machi-koro.
2. **Buttons moved into the dock (`DockSlot`).** They render outside the renderer root, so the tests now look in the page or in `.tbl-dock`:
   - race-galaxy, space-base.
3. **Real UI bugs**, fixed in the product:
   - azul and hive clicks were blocked;
   - the patchwork quilt was invisible;
   - the go acceptance status was missing.

Test races were fixed too:
- The kingdomino and cryptid tutorial hints arrive after the bot's reply; the tests now wait for them.
- The carcassonne zoomed board can hold spots outside the visible window; the test dispatches the click.
- A six-nimmt click during the reveal animation is retried.
- The bohnanza game loop is bounded by time instead of 900 moves.

More fixes found while re-running:
- The machi-koro TV-station and landmark buttons, and the space-base dice choices, moved into the dock. The tests now look in `.tbl-dock`.
- The bohnanza status («اول یک مزرعه را برداشت کنید» and others) was not shown anywhere in the frame. It is now in the rail (product fix).
- The el-dorado test now copes with moments when no seat is marked.

After the stage-fit recapture, a few more fixes:
- **ticket-to-ride:** a route that cannot be built no longer takes the click meant for its buildable twin. This showed up once the map was fitted to the stage height.
- **cryptid:** restored the `cr-cube` hook on the cube art.
- **azul:** the factory ring box is LTR, so the centre pool sits on the same axis as the factories. In RTL, `inset-inline-start: 50%` combined with a physical translate pushed it to one side.
- **catan:** the board cap is now `--zb-aspect: 0.72`, which leaves room for the resource hand.
- **Known, not fixed:** the ticket-to-ride starting-ticket panel and the azul own board still need a scroll inside the stage at 1440×900.
